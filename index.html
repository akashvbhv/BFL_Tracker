Mastering the Ledger System

This application follows the Bajaj Finserv Overdraft financial logic: Daily interest accrual with monthly capitalization.

1. Member Creation

Add members in the sidebar. Note: Adding or Deleting members requires the Master Password to prevent data loss.

2. Daily Interest Calculation

Interest is calculated every 24 hours based on the closing principal balance using the Global Rate set in the ledger.

3. EMI on Interest (Compounding)

On the 1st of every month, the accumulated daily interest is converted into an EMI on Interest entry and added to the principal balance.

4. Corrections & Recalculation

If you edit an entry from the past, the system will instantly re-process the entire timeline to ensure the current balance is 100% accurate.
